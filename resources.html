Mandatory Compliance Forms (Target: Oct 22)

  • Player Medical Information Form (PDF)
    Must be collected for every player and kept securely in the bench binder at all times.
  • Bench Staff Code of Conduct (PDF)
    Mandatory form for all coaches, managers, and trainers on the roster.
  • Parent / Guardian Code of Conduct (PDF)
    To be signed by parents/guardians at the pre-season parent meeting.
  • TAMHA Jersey Agreement & Signout Form (PDF)
    Jersey distribution agreement, care guidelines, and deposit collection record.
  • Hockey Nova Scotia Injury Report Form (PDF)
    Must be filed within 7 days of any injury requiring medical attention or missed ice time.
  • TAMHA Criminal Record Checks (CRC) & Screening
    Screening procedures and requirements for all bench staff and volunteers.
