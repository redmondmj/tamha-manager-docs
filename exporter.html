📋 How to Import into GrayJay Central

  1. Select your team in the dropdown above and click "Download Official GrayJay Import (.xlsx)".
  2. Log into GrayJay Central (or your GrayJay Leagues Team Admin).
  3. Navigate to Schedule > Practices > Import (or Team Events Import).
  4. Upload the downloaded Excel file. GrayJay will parse all dates, start/end times, arenas, and Full Ice settings automatically!
  5. Review and hit Confirm. Your entire team calendar is populated with zero manual typing.
