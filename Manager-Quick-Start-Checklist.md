# Quick-Glance Checklist: Essential Manager Requirements

This is your bare-bones, chronological checklist for getting your team set up, compliant, and on the ice. For full details on any of these tasks, follow the links to the [Manager Detailed Responsibilities Guide](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md).

### Phase 1: Immediate Tasks (Days 1-3 Post-Team Selection)
- [ ] **Team Manager Onboarding & GrayJay Access:** The Head Coach is assigned an official TAMHA email account first and asked to confirm their designated Team Manager. Once confirmed, provide your details (Full Name, Date of Birth, and HCR #) to receive your official manager email account and GrayJay manager access. *(Roster Guardrails: Rosters sync from registrations; never manually add/release players or add extra admins. Tell parents to add contacts via GrayJay Pay).* ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Submit Full Bench Staff & APs to TAMHA (Comms & Tech):** Email your complete coaching and bench staff list (Assistant Coaches, Safety Rep, Trainers) with full legal names, assigned roles, D.O.B., and HCR #s directly to Comms & Tech at `tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca` ASAP. Team managers cannot add staff directly in GrayJay—TAMHA must link them to your roster. *(Rep teams: Also submit your list of AP players to be linked).* ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **HCR Staff Profile & Waiver Sign-Off:** All bench staff must sign into [register.hockeycanada.ca](https://register.hockeycanada.ca) to verify account info and sign mandatory annual waivers (often the #1 blocker holding up team roster approvals). New staff without an HCR profile must register under TAMHA as a new coach/volunteer first to generate their profile and start clinics. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Team Banking Registration:** Submit signer details via the [TAMHA Bank Signer Intake Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/19KlBkgZ-YiAcxqpsXk-KPW9Ud8RgKGBi9l2Nf4iTpz4/viewform). VP Finance generates and submits the official authorization letter directly to Mosaik Credit Union on your behalf (managers do not send letters or visit the branch). Signature cards are completed digitally via DocuSign. **All signers must complete DocuSign before online banking credentials can be released (if one person doesn't sign, it holds up the whole team's account).** **Do not collect parent funds until this is active.** Two signers are required. *(Cash Handling Rule: Always count and track cash with a second team official as a witness, issue immediate receipts, and deposit promptly).* ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))
- [ ] **Secure Game Jerseys & Team Socks:** Email `equipment@trurominorhockey.ca` immediately to make arrangements for jersey pickup and inquire about availability of matching team socks (teams often play exhibition games right away). Assign jerseys, collect deposits, and check with the Head Coach regarding practice jersey requirements. ([See Team Culture & Apparel](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#4-team-culture--nice-to-haves))

### Phase 2: Administrative Setup (Week 1)
- [ ] **Collect Player Medical Forms:** Secure signed Medical Forms and keep copies in the bench binder at all times! ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Identify Target Tournaments:** Identify target tournaments, register immediately or mark your calendar and submit early payments to secure spots. ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Jersey Deposits & Sign-outs:** Collect $150/player (all teams, both Rep and House). *Must be refunded at year-end upon safe return.* *(Note: Some divisions receive sponsored jerseys and do not collect jersey deposits. If unsure, confirm with your division representative or email `info@trurominorhockey.ca`.)* ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))
- [ ] **Host Parent Meeting & Budget Approval Vote:** Pitch the budget (any out-of-pocket parent fees/cash calls **require a >75% majority approval vote**; present the $0 out-of-pocket fundraising plan for C-League), detail the **24-Hour Rule**, enforce the **Hockey Canada Dressing Room Policy** (players arrive with base layer on & mandatory Rule of Two), share the team calendar **"SUBSCRIBE"** link and **GrayJay Teams App** (chat & RSVP), align on coach music preferences (speaker & acceptable playlists), review player equipment safety standards (CSA helmet stickers/lifecycles, ear loops/straps, BNQ neck guards), and recruit volunteers (Treasurer, Safety Person, Dressing Room Monitors, and a **Designated Backup Timekeeper** with TAMHA pay). ([See Team Culture](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#4-team-culture--nice-to-haves))
- [ ] **Player Equipment & Safety Verification:** Review player gear against Hockey Canada standards: inspect helmets for valid unexpired CSA stickers, intact ear loops, and secure chin straps, and verify every player has a BNQ-certified throat protector. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Collect Forms:** Secure signed Codes of Conduct, Social Media Policies, Bench Code of Conduct, Fair Play Policy, and Jersey Agreements. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Follow Up on Player Transfers to TAMHA:** Identify any players who recently moved to the Truro area and played for another minor hockey association last season. Ensure parents submit the [Parent Declaration Form](https://trurominorhockey.ca/uploads/truromha/source/0/Parents/Transfers%20to%20TAMHA/Parent%20Declaration%20Form.pdf) to `info@trurominorhockey.ca` so their HCR profile can be transferred. Unfinalized transfers will hold up official roster approval. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))

### Phase 3: Early Season Action Items (Weeks 2-4)
- [ ] **Verify Certifications/CRCs (Strict Deadline: Dec 1st):** Email `info@trurominorhockey.ca` to ensure all bench staff have Respect in Sport, Criminal Record Checks / VSCs (done at local police department with TAMHA letter and 2 IDs), and required HNS courses before December 1st. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Audit League Schedule for Tournament Conflicts (Rep Teams):** League schedulers (Central Minor, North Conference, NRHF) **do not** check tournament dates. Cross-reference your league schedule against tournament weekends immediately and notify opposing managers **weeks in advance** to initiate reschedules. ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Solicit Sponsorships:** Distribute letters to target community sponsors to help subsidize budgets. ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))
- [ ] **Collect Rep Fees:** Collect team Rep Fees ($9,500 standard / $11,500 U13 AAA, split Nov 30 & Jan 31; includes mandatory jersey fund). ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))
- [ ] **Exhibition Game Setup (Out-of-League):** For exhibition games outside your league, follow the 6-step SOP: book opponent via GrayJay Central, secure ice, enter as "Out of League" / "To Be Announced" (or Scheduled Event for away games), submit mandatory Spordle Travel Permit (1 week prior; **must title as: `Exhibition Game - [Opposing Team Name]`** per Hockey NS; unapproved permits risk suspensions), request refs/timekeepers via RIC before Monday evening cutoff, and pre-fill physical paper game sheets (GrayJay doesn't support live stats for out-of-league play; enter score in GrayJay by end of day). ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))

### Phase 4: Ongoing / Weekly Rhythm
- [ ] **Roster Sign-Offs (Paperless SOP):** Sign off on the game roster in GrayJay mobile *before* puck drop (select game &rarr; Home/Visiting Sign Off &rarr; choose goalie/bench staff &rarr; **turn phone sideways to sign**). Mark late players absent or update as needed. ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Affiliate Tracking & Roster Rules:** Ensure both Head Coaches confirm when an affiliate is called up, email `tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca` with player details, change AP from "NOT DRESSED" to "DRESSED" with jersey number, verify APs do not exceed regular roster size cap, and track total game count (max 10). ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Travel Permits (Spordle):** Submit permits for **ALL** non-regular season games (Exhibition and Tournaments) 1 week in advance. **Mandatory Title Format:** Always put the opponent name in the permit title (e.g., `Exhibition Game - [Opposing Team Name]` or `Tournament - [Name]`) for both Home and Away games so Hockey NS can approve without queue delays. ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Injury Reports:** If a player requires medical attention, complete and submit a Hockey Nova Scotia Injury Report. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Weekly Updates:** Send a weekly email/GrayJay Team chat with the upcoming schedule, arrival times, and action items. **Always put the Division/Team name in the email subject.** Remind parents to RSVP attendance on the GrayJay Teams app. ([See Team Culture](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#4-team-culture--nice-to-haves))
