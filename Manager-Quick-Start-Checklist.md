# Quick-Glance Checklist: Essential Manager Requirements

This is your bare-bones, chronological checklist for getting your team set up, compliant, and on the ice. For full details on any of these tasks, follow the links to the [Manager Detailed Responsibilities Guide](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md).

### Phase 1: Immediate Tasks (Days 1-3 Post-Team Selection)
- [ ] **HCR Staff Profile & Waiver Sign-Off:** All bench staff must sign into [register.hockeycanada.ca](https://register.hockeycanada.ca) to verify account info and sign mandatory annual waivers (often the #1 blocker holding up team roster approvals). New staff without an HCR profile must register under TAMHA as a new coach/volunteer first to generate their profile and start clinics. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **GrayJay Account Setup:** Email `tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca` with your Name, Position, Date of Birth, and HCR # to get your manager access set up. ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Team Banking Registration:** Submit signer details via the [TAMHA Bank Signer Intake Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/19KlBkgZ-YiAcxqpsXk-KPW9Ud8RgKGBi9l2Nf4iTpz4/viewform) to request an official authorization letter from VP Finance. **Do not collect parent funds until this is active.** Two signers are required. ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))
- [ ] **Secure Game Jerseys & Team Socks:** Pick up and assign team jerseys and order/obtain game socks immediately—teams often play exhibition games right away. Check with the Head Coach regarding practice jersey requirements (team-supplied vs. player-supplied colors). ([See Team Culture & Apparel](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#4-team-culture--nice-to-haves))

### Phase 2: Administrative Setup (Week 1)
- [ ] **Collect Player Medical Forms:** Secure signed Medical Forms and keep copies in the bench binder at all times! ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Identify Target Tournaments:** Identify target tournaments, register immediately or mark your calendar and submit early payments to secure spots. ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Jersey Deposits & Sign-outs:** Collect $150/player (Rep) or $50/player (House). *Must be refunded at year-end upon safe return.* ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))
- [ ] **Host Parent Meeting:** Pitch the budget, detail the **24-Hour Rule**, enforce the **Rule of Two** (dressing room conduct & no dropping kids off early/unsupervised), align on coach music preferences (speaker & acceptable playlists), and assign volunteer roles (Treasurer, Safety Person, Dressing Room Monitors). ([See Team Culture](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#4-team-culture--nice-to-haves))
- [ ] **Collect Forms:** Secure signed Codes of Conduct, Social Media Policies, Bench Code of Conduct, Fair Play Policy, and Jersey Agreements. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))

### Phase 3: Early Season Action Items (Weeks 2-4)
- [ ] **Verify Certifications/CRCs:** Email `info@trurominorhockey.ca` to ensure all bench staff have Respect in Sport, Criminal Record Checks, and required HNS courses. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Solicit Sponsorships:** Distribute letters to target community sponsors to help subsidize budgets. ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))
- [ ] **Collect Rep Fees:** Collect team Rep Fees (if applicable). ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))
- [ ] **Collect Jersey Fund Contribution:** Collect the mandatory Jersey Fund contribution per team. ([See Financial Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#2-financial-management))

### Phase 4: Ongoing / Weekly Rhythm
- [ ] **Roster Sign-Offs:** Sign off on the game roster in GrayJay Leagues (or via the Teams App link) *before* puck drop. ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Affiliate Tracking:** Ensure both Head Coaches confirm when an affiliate is called up, email `tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca` with player details, and track their total game count (max 10). ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Travel Permits (Spordle):** Submit permits for **ALL** non-regular season games (Exhibition and Tournaments) well in advance. ([See Roster & Game Management](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#3-roster--game-management))
- [ ] **Injury Reports:** If a player requires medical attention, complete and submit a Hockey Nova Scotia Injury Report. ([See Safety & Compliance](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#1-safety--compliance))
- [ ] **Weekly Updates:** Send a weekly email/GrayJay Team chat with the upcoming schedule, arrival times, and action items. **Always put the Division/Team name in the email subject.** ([See Team Culture](./Manager-Detailed-Responsibilities-Guide.md#4-team-culture--nice-to-haves))
