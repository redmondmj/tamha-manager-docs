# Manager Detailed Responsibilities Guide

This comprehensive guide outlines the primary responsibilities and "nice-to-have" tasks for team managers throughout the hockey season. It acts as a detailed reference manual, providing context and instruction for the items listed in your Quick-Start Checklist. It is ordered by level of critical importance.

---

## 1. Safety & Compliance

*   **HCR Profile, Annual Waivers & New Staff Onboarding (Immediate):** Before team rosters can be approved by Hockey Nova Scotia, every bench staff member (Head Coach, Assistants, Managers, Safety Reps) must sign into their Hockey Canada Registry (HCR 3.0) account at [register.hockeycanada.ca](https://register.hockeycanada.ca).
    *   **Returning Staff:** Log in, verify your personal/contact details are current, and ensure all critical annual waivers are signed off. Unsigned waivers are the single most frequent issue that holds up official team roster approvals.
    *   **New Staff (No Existing HCR Profile):** Any coach or volunteer without an existing HCR profile must visit [register.hockeycanada.ca](https://register.hockeycanada.ca) and sign up with *Truro Area Minor Hockey Association (TAMHA)* as a new coach/volunteer. This creates your profile and HCR # so TAMHA can add you to the roster and you can begin your required clinics.
*   **Certifications & Vulnerable Sector Checks (Strict Deadline: December 1st):** Verify coaching and bench staff waivers, certifications, and [CRCs](https://trurominorhockey.ca/pages/4117/Records-Checks/) by emailing TAMHA at [info@trurominorhockey.ca](mailto:info@trurominorhockey.ca) to request the current status for your team. A full list of required coaching clinics can be found on the [Hockey Nova Scotia Coaching Requirements](https://hockeynovascotia.ca/coach/coaching-requirements) page.
    *   **December 1st Hard Deadline:** All bench staff credentials, certifications, and Vulnerable Sector Checks (VSC) must be fully completed and approved by **December 1st**. Uncertified staff will be removed from rosters after this date.
    *   **VSC Procedure:** Volunteers complete their VSC at their local police department by bringing two (2) pieces of government identification and the official TAMHA Volunteer Letter. Remind volunteers to complete this early in the fall, as processing can take additional time if fingerprint verification is triggered.
*   **Hockey Canada Dressing Room Policy & Mandatory "Rule of Two":**
    *   **Inclusive Team Space & Base Layer Requirement:** Under Hockey Canada's updated Dressing Room Policy, dressing rooms are designated inclusive team spaces. Players arriving at the arena are required to have, at minimum, their **base layer / undergear already on** (e.g., shorts and t-shirt, or base layer compression top and bottoms).
    *   **Rule of Two (Mandatory at All Times):** Two screened, certified team officials (or approved dressing room monitors) must be present in the dressing room or immediately outside the open doorway whenever players are present. At no time should a single adult be alone with a player in the dressing room. Refer to the full [Hockey Canada Dressing Room Policy (PDF)](https://cdn.hockeycanada.ca/hockey-canada/Hockey-Programs/Safety/Downloads/dressing-room-policy-e.pdf).
*   **Player Transfers to TAMHA (Association Transfers):**
    *   **Who Needs to Transfer:** If a family on your team has recently moved to the Truro area and their player participated with another minor hockey association last season, they must complete the official [Parent Declaration Form (PDF)](https://trurominorhockey.ca/uploads/truromha/source/0/Parents/Transfers%20to%20TAMHA/Parent%20Declaration%20Form.pdf). This document officially declares and verifies their change of address so that Hockey Canada Registry (HCR 3.0) can approve their inter-association transfer to TAMHA.
    *   **Submission:** Parents must send the signed form directly to TAMHA administration at [info@trurominorhockey.ca](mailto:info@trurominorhockey.ca).
    *   **Manager Follow-Up:** Review your roster early. If you have any players who transferred from another association, proactively follow up with their parents to ensure this form has been submitted. **Unapproved transfers will block the player from being approved on your official roster and prevent them from appearing on electronic game sheets or travel permits.**
    *   *(Note: Players living in another association's jurisdiction who tried out for and were selected to a Truro Rep team do NOT need this form; they will be asked to register with TAMHA directly once team selection is finalized. For full details, see the [TAMHA Transfers Policy Page](https://trurominorhockey.ca/pages/4128/Transfers-to-TAMHA/)).*
*   **Form Collection (Target: October 22nd):** Collect all mandatory documentation at the start of the season. All forms and master templates are maintained in the centralized [TAMHA Google Drive Document Library](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/12YASkGfKkvR-mMcALXcmY_Asi2gOZOIA?usp=sharing). Download or copy official templates below:
    *   [Player Medical Information Form (PDF)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bnN2W7085233yWdCNspIyH5GhJqPQqFp/view?usp=sharing) — *Keep physical copies on the bench at all times*
    *   [Parent / Guardian Code of Conduct (PDF)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-_LmgBCZhs0KAa1Yz1YvDJseORyKgJAo/view?usp=sharing) — *Includes Social Media & Fair Play policies*
    *   [Bench Staff Code of Conduct (PDF)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AN7zjP8WjH4hlKw6nzTQwow-5pIwnMze/view?usp=sharing) — *Required for all coaches, managers & trainers*
    *   [TAMHA Jersey Agreement & Signout Form (PDF)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CmuPM-xuvLECR6Q-N2AwIBneRRuuiMcp/view?usp=sharing) — *Collect alongside jersey deposit*
*   **Injury Reports & Emergency First Responder Hand-Off:**
    *   Complete and file a [Hockey Nova Scotia Injury Report Form (PDF)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bZhWevF90bXPvkqwCABfn0RAakzdPkAt/view?usp=sharing) with Hockey Nova Scotia anytime a player requires medical attention or misses ice time due to injury (must be submitted within 7 days of the incident).
    *   **Crucial Bench Binder Protocol:** Always keep several blank copies of the Injury Report Form along with the physical copies of all signed **Player Medical Information Forms** in your team bench binder at every game and practice.
    *   **Emergency Transport Protocol:** In the event of a serious injury where paramedics / first responders are dispatched or a player is taken to urgent care by a parent/guardian, immediately hand a physical copy of that player's Medical Information Form (providing health card number, emergency contacts, known allergies, and medical conditions) and a blank Injury Report Form directly to the first responders or guardian. This guarantees emergency room staff have instant access to vital medical data, and the attending physician can complete the medical section of the injury report before leaving the clinic.
*   **Emergency Action Plan (EAP):** Designate an on-ice Charge Person and off-ice Call Person (often the Safety Rep or Manager) to execute the Emergency Action Plan during games and practices. Maintain arena-specific emergency protocols and 911 dispatch scripts in your binder. (See [Arena EAP Template (Google Doc)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1e-gegg752dl0q0P_FvMg3L1XufUnaEAx/copy)).
*   **Equipment Standards & Player Safety Compliance (Hockey Canada Guidelines):**
    *   **CSA-Certified Helmets & Expiration Lifecycles:** Referees, coaches, and safety reps strictly enforce helmet regulations on the ice. Every player and goaltender helmet must display an intact, legible **CSA certification sticker** that has not passed its expiration lifecycle.
    *   **Hardware, Chin Straps & Ear Loops:** Inspect all helmets before the first practice and game. Ear loops and chin straps must be intact, properly fastened, and cannot be cut, unclipped, or altered. Screws must be tight and secure.
    *   **Cracks & Prohibited Modifications:** Helmets with visible cracks, structural compromises, or missing pieces are strictly prohibited and cannot be worn on the ice. In accordance with CSA regulations, painting helmets with non-approved paints or affixing unauthorized decals can degrade protective plastics and voids certification. Any damaged or expired helmet must be retired and replaced immediately.
    *   **BNQ-Certified Neck Laceration Protectors (Throat Protectors):** All players and goaltenders must wear a BNQ-certified throat protector during all sanctioned on-ice activities (practices, warm-ups, and games). The protector must fit snugly around the base of the neck and cannot be altered, cut, or trimmed in any manner.
    *   **Mouthguards:** Properly fitted mouthguards are required/recommended per HNS regulations.
    *   **Official Safety Resource:** Direct parents and coaches to [Hockey Canada Equipment Essentials](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/safety/essentials/equipment) for detailed fitting instructions, maintenance standards, and visual certification guides. Review these requirements explicitly during your pre-season parent meeting so families have ample time to replace non-compliant gear.

## 2. Financial Management

*   **Team Budgeting & Distribution (Google Drive "Shared with me"):**
    *   **Official Pre-Populated Team Sheet:** For the 2026–2027 season, each team's official operating budget spreadsheet is automatically generated by VP Finance during bank onboarding and shared directly with your designated Manager and Treasurer in Google Drive under [**Shared with me**](https://drive.google.com/drive/shared-with-me). You do not need to request access or build formulas from scratch—it is pre-populated with your team's division, roster count formula, and fixed association Rep fees ($9,500 Rep / $11,500 AAA / $0 C-League).
    *   **🚨 Already Started an Excel Sheet? Don't Panic!** *For the 2026–2027 season, if you have already created a budget in another spreadsheet, please don't re-type it from scratch. We will be happy to merge your existing numbers into the official format for you!* Simply email what you have to TAMHA Finance ([vpfinance@trurominorhockey.ca](mailto:vpfinance@trurominorhockey.ca)) or your manager coordinator.
*   **Parent Fee Assessments & The >75% Majority Vote Rule (TAMHA & HNS Policy):**
    *   **Mandatory Parent Vote:** Under TAMHA and Hockey Nova Scotia regulations, a coach or manager cannot unilaterally impose out-of-pocket parent fees or team cash calls on families. Any preliminary team budget that levies mandatory parent contributions beyond baseline registration (e.g., to cover Rep fee shortfalls, tournament entries, team tracksuits, or extra exhibition ice) must be presented to the parent group at your introductory meeting and **approved by at least a >75% majority vote**.
    *   **Strategic Two-Track Presentation (Especially for C-League):** For C-League (House) teams paying $0 in association rep fees, all discretionary team extras (tournaments, hoodies, year-end parties) must be funded either through fundraising/sponsors or direct parent cash calls. Present two clear options to your parent group:
        *   *Option A (The $0 Out-of-Pocket Plan via Fundraising):* Present your total projected team expenses (e.g., $2,500 for two tournaments and team events) alongside an achievable fundraising plan (bottle drives, 50/50, banner sponsors) showing a net parent assessment of **$0.00**.
        *   *Option B (The Direct Cash Call):* If parents prefer not to participate in sales or fundraising, offer a direct per-player contribution (e.g., $100–$150), subject to the required >75% secret ballot or show-of-hands vote.
    *   **Why This Works:** Presenting these two options transparently eliminates friction, gives families complete ownership of their season costs, and motivates parent volunteers to step up and lead fundraising so that out-of-pocket costs stay at zero.
*   **Team Banking Step-by-Step SOP (Mosaik Credit Union):**
    *   **Step 1 — Submit Signer Intake Form:** Submit signing officer details directly via the [TAMHA Bank Signer Intake Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/19KlBkgZ-YiAcxqpsXk-KPW9Ud8RgKGBi9l2Nf4iTpz4/viewform). You must provide the full legal names, positions (e.g., Manager, Treasurer), phone numbers, and email addresses for both designated signing officers.
    *   **Step 2 — Executive Authorization Letter (Handled Directly by VP Finance):** VP Finance (Joe Zappia) reviews the intake submission, generates the official TAMHA corporate bank authorization letter, and delivers it directly to the commercial services team at Mosaik Credit Union (Truro branch). Managers and treasurers do NOT need to request, pick up, or deliver letters to the bank.
    *   **Step 3 — Digital Signature Cards via DocuSign (No Branch Visit):** Mosaik Credit Union actively discourages in-person branch visits. Signature cards are generated digitally and emailed directly to designated signing officers via **DocuSign**. **Important:** *All* designated signers must review and electronically sign the authorization package before online banking information can be released. If even one person delays or fails to sign, it completely holds up the account setup process for the entire team.
    *   **Step 4 — Online Banking Credentials & Dual-Authorization Setup:** Once all signers have completed DocuSign, Mosaik Credit Union emails team account details and a **Personal Access Code (PAC)** directly to signers to activate digital banking (online banking info is held until all signatures are complete). **Dual-authorization is mandatory**: electronic e-transfers require one signing officer to initiate and the second signing officer to log in and approve before funds are released. Cash or cheque withdrawals require two signing officers present or two authorized signatures on physical cheques. *(For banking questions: [vpfinance@trurominorhockey.ca](mailto:vpfinance@trurominorhockey.ca))*
*   **TAMHA Rep Fees (Rep Teams Only):**
    *   **Standard Rep Teams (AA, A, B):** **$9,500 per team** for the 2026–2027 season, payable to TAMHA in two equal installments ($4,750 on November 30 and $4,750 on January 31).
    *   **U13 AAA Rep Teams:** **$11,500 per team** ($5,750 per installment: Nov 30 and Jan 31) due to additional scheduled dedicated ice allocations.
    *   **Avoiding Sticker Shock (Comprehensive Inclusions):** While $9,500 ($11,500 for U13 AAA) is higher than in prior seasons, it is critical to clarify to parents to avoid "sticker shock" that **this fee now directly includes the mandatory annual jersey/sweater fund** (previously an additional $550 charge per team), **HNS and conference league fees**, and **two (2) pairs of official Bearcats game socks per player** (Home Crimson & Away White, supplied at equipment pickup). Teams do *not* pay separate invoices for sweater funds, league fees, or game socks.
    *   **C-League (House) Teams:** Pay **$0.00** in association rep fees (core ice and league operations are covered by baseline TAMHA player registration).
    *   **Two-Stage Cash-Flow Schedule:** Rep fees are split into two equal payments (November 30 and January 31) giving managers and parent groups time to solicit community sponsorships and collect initial parent installments before the second payment is due.
*   **Jersey Deposits:** Collect a $150/player Jersey Deposit for Rep teams ($50 for House teams) alongside the signed Jersey Agreement. Post-dated cheques (dated **May 15, 2027** payable to *TAMHA*) are ideal. If a family does not have cheques, an acceptable alternative is cash or an e-transfer held in trust. **Important:** It is the team's responsibility to hold and return uncashed cheques/cash, or refund e-transfers in full at the end of the season when both jerseys are returned in good condition.
    *   **Sponsored Jersey Exception:** Some divisions receive fully sponsored jerseys and do *not* need to collect jersey deposits. If you are unsure whether your division qualifies, please confirm with your division representative or contact TAMHA at [info@trurominorhockey.ca](mailto:info@trurominorhockey.ca).
*   **Cash Handling, Dual-Control & Tracking (Laurie's "Witness Rule"):**
    *   **Always Have a Witness:** When collecting cash payments from parents or sponsors (for team fees, jersey deposits, apparel orders, game socks, or fundraising tickets), never accept, handle, or count cash alone. Always ensure a second team official (e.g., Manager + Treasurer, or Manager + Assistant Coach) is present as an active witness.
    *   **Dual Counting & Immediate Receipting:** Count the money together in front of the payer to confirm the exact total. Immediately record the transaction in your team finance spreadsheet or ledger, and issue an immediate written receipt or confirmation email recording the date, player/payer name, exact cash amount, and purpose.
    *   **Segregated & Sealed Storage:** Place cash collections immediately into a dedicated, labeled envelope or lockbox marked with the date, total amount, payer names, and initials of both team officials who counted it. Never leave loose cash in hockey bags, car gloveboxes, or unattended at the rink.
    *   **Prompt Bank Deposit:** Deposit all cash collections into the team's Mosaik Credit Union account as soon as reasonably possible. Never co-mingle team cash collections with personal cash or use cash for out-of-pocket team purchases before depositing and logging it.
*   **Sponsorship & Fundraising Management:**
    *   **Sponsorship Outreach:** Distribute the official [Team Sponsorship Request Letter (Google Doc)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1g0c2JTK6_G58kDTBOPLDfQfnlHICo4sl/copy) to local businesses and corporate partners early in the fall.
    *   **Dressing Room Banners & Sponsor Boards:** If your team creates a dressing room door banner or tournament sponsor board, instruct sponsors to supply **high-resolution vector logos** (`.EPS`, `.SVG`, `.AI`) or transparent 300+ DPI `.PNG` files. Low-resolution website screenshots will result in blurry, pixelated banner prints.
    *   **Official Sponsorship Receipts:** Provide written or emailed sponsorship receipts promptly to every business contributor detailing the team name, date, contribution amount, and sponsor business name. Remind businesses that minor sports team sponsorships qualify as legitimate advertising and promotional business expenses.
    *   **Pre-Account Holding Balances:** If sponsorship cheques, cash, or e-transfers arrive before your Mosaik Credit Union team account is fully operational, hold all cheques uncashed and keep cash/e-transfers segregated in a dedicated team holding ledger. **Never co-mingle team sponsorship or player fee funds with personal bank accounts.** Deposit all funds immediately once the team account is active.
    *   **Budget Tracking & Allocation:** Record all incoming sponsorships and fundraising proceeds directly in the **Income** tab (Sections 1.0 & 2.0) of your official team budget spreadsheet in Google Drive ([**Shared with me**](https://drive.google.com/drive/shared-with-me)). Track contributor business names, sponsorship tiers, receipt numbers, and whether funds offset general team overhead (reducing equal parent fee assessments) or apply to specific player ledgers per your team's documented policy.
*   **Financial Reporting Deadlines:** 
    *   Submit Preliminary Budget to VP Finance by **Oct 30th**.
    *   Provide Mid-Season Financial Statement to Parents by **Dec 31st**.
    *   Submit Interim Budget to VP Finance by **Jan 15th**.
    *   Submit Final Budget to VP Finance by **Mar 31st**.
    *   Provide Final Financial Statement to Parents by **Apr 15th**.

## 3. Roster & Game Management

*   **Team Staff Onboarding & GrayJay Manager Access:** Following team selection, each Head Coach is assigned an official TAMHA email account (e.g., `<division>hc@trurominorhockey.ca`) and contacted by TAMHA to confirm their designated Team Manager. Once the manager is confirmed and provides their verification details (Full Legal Name, Date of Birth, and Hockey Canada Registry / HCR #), TAMHA will provision an official team manager email account (e.g., `<division>mgr@trurominorhockey.ca`) and grant GrayJay manager access.
    *   **GrayJay Roster Guardrails (Strict Compliance):** Team rosters are populated directly from registration data:
        *   **Allowed:** Managers and coaches may add jersey numbers, update player positions, and view contacts.
        *   **Adding Parent Contacts (Preferred Method):** Instruct parents/guardians to log directly into **GrayJay Pay** to add secondary family contacts to their player profile themselves. Only enter contacts manually if the parent experiences technical difficulty and has granted explicit permission.
        *   **STRICT PROHIBITIONS (DO NOT):** Never manually add players to a roster (creates duplicates that break sync with Hockey Canada Registry), never release players, never add team staff (contact TAMHA), and never add extra team admins (admins are strictly limited to Head Coaches and Managers unless authorized by Comms & Tech). Violating these rules can result in revocation of admin access.
*   **Jersey Sorting, Distribution & Immediate Roster Sync:**
    *   **Equipment Pickup & Sock Availability:** Email `equipment@trurominorhockey.ca` to make arrangements for jersey pickup (Home Crimson and Away White sets) from equipment storage and inquire about current stock/availability of matching TAMHA game socks.
    *   **Sorting & Inspection:** Before meeting with players, unpack both team sets (Home Crimson and Away White). Organize jerseys by size (Small, Medium, Large, XL, Goalie Cut) and lay them out in numerical order to confirm each number has a matching pair in the exact same cut.
    *   **Allocation & Sizing:** Assign sizes logically based on player build and position (ensure goalies and defensemen wearing bulky shoulder padding receive appropriately cut sweaters).
    *   **Deposits & Signout:** Do not release jerseys without first receiving the signed [TAMHA Jersey Signout Form](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CmuPM-xuvLECR6Q-N2AwIBneRRuuiMcp/view?usp=sharing) and the deposit ($150 Rep / $50 House post-dated to May 15, 2027). Distribute matching TAMHA game socks at the same time.
    *   **Immediate GrayJay & HCR Roster Number Sync:** The moment jersey numbers are assigned, immediately log into GrayJay Leagues / Teams and input every player's official jersey number. Electronic game sheets pull numbers directly from your roster; updating numbers immediately prevents game-day delays and scrambles before your first game.
    *   **Jersey Care Guidelines:** Instruct parents to wash jerseys inside-out in cold water on gentle cycle and **hang dry only**. Never use a dryer (dryer heat will melt heat-pressed crests, warp numbers, and damage fabric). Keep jerseys away from exposed velcro on gloves and equipment bags to prevent fabric snagging. Remind players that game jerseys are strictly for official games—never to be worn for practices or street hockey.
*   **Affiliate Player (AP) Tracking & Roster Rules:** All affiliate player call-ups must be pre-approved by both Head Coaches prior to any game. Once confirmed, email `tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca` with the player's full name and D.O.B. so they can be temporarily added to the game roster.
    *   **GrayJay Status ("NOT DRESSED" Default):** Affiliates added to your roster automatically default to **"NOT DRESSED"** in GrayJay. If an AP is playing, the manager must actively switch them to **"DRESSED"** and assign their jersey number and position during pre-game sign-off. An unidentified player on the ice will result in referee enforcement action.
    *   **Strict Roster Cap (No Roster Padding):** Affiliates can **only** be used to replace absent rostered players—**never to supplement your roster above your regular team size**. (For example, a team with 14 assigned players cannot use APs to bring their game-day bench to 15–17 players).
    *   **10-Game Limit:** Managers must strictly log every affiliate appearance; players are subject to a **maximum of 10 affiliate games** per season (excluding tournament play).
*   **Booking Opponents:** Use [GrayJay Central](https://grayjaycentral.ca/) to find teams from other associations in your division/tier and access manager/coach contact info. Use the search filters to check for schedule openings. Click the team crest and open the Staff tab to retrieve direct phone and email contact details.
*   **Exhibition Games Step-by-Step SOP (Home & Away):**
    *   **Step 1 — Arrange Opponent:** Contact the opposing manager via GrayJay Central to agree on game date, time, venue, and cost sharing (customary protocol: home team supplies ice, refs, and timekeepers; away team hosts a reciprocal game, or costs are split 50/50).
    *   **Step 2 — Confirm Ice Allocation:** For Home games, confirm your assigned ice slot with the TAMHA ice scheduler or book extra ice at the Rath Eastlink Community Centre (RECC) or Colchester Legion Stadium. For Away games, confirm arena location, pad, and dressing room arrival times with the host manager.
    *   **Step 3 — Create Game or Scheduled Event in GrayJay:**
        *   **Home Exhibition Games:** Log into GrayJay Leagues / Teams manager portal &rarr; Select "GAMES" &rarr; Season: **"EXHIBITION"** &rarr; Click **"ADD GAME"**. Ensure date, time, and AM/PM are correct, and verify Home Association is set to *Truro Area Minor Hockey Association*. For out-of-league opponents (e.g., North Conference vs. Central Minor), select **"To Be Announced"** (TBA) and manually type the opponent's full association and team name underneath.
        *   **Away Exhibition Games (GrayJay Teams):** The home team is responsible for game creation in GrayJay. To have an away exhibition game appear on your team's GrayJay calendar, the away manager must create a **Scheduled Event** under the **"SCHEDULE"** menu (not the Games menu) reflecting the exhibition game.
        *   **Away Game Exception (Non-GrayJay Teams):** If playing an away game against a team that does *not* use GrayJay (e.g. out-of-province or independent), the away manager can enter the game directly under the "GAMES" menu.
    *   **Step 4 — Submit Spordle Travel Permit (Mandatory Naming Convention):** Hockey Nova Scotia mandates an approved travel permit for **all exhibition games (both home and away)** and tournaments. Submit your permit request via the Spordle portal at [https://myaccount.spordle.com/manage/permit](https://myaccount.spordle.com/manage/permit) at least **1 week in advance** (48 to 72 hours minimum) to allow for association and HNS approval.
        *   **Spordle Navigation:** Log in &rarr; Click **"TASKS"** on left menu &rarr; Click **"TRAVEL PERMITS"** &rarr; Click **"ADD"** &rarr; Complete required fields. (Note: A status of "Pending" is normal; permits are routinely approved without a distinct permit number being emailed back).
        *   **🚨 Mandatory Title Naming Convention (Hockey Nova Scotia / Terry Nichol Rule):** When submitting your travel permit in Spordle, **always put the opposing team's name directly in the permit title**, formatted as:
            > **`Exhibition Game - [Opposing Team Name]`**  
            > *(e.g., `Exhibition Game - Pictou County Crushers Red` or `Exhibition Game - Cumberland Ramblers`)*
        *   **Why this is mandatory (Home AND Away):** Even for away games where the opponent is listed as the host team, Hockey NS regional directors (Terry Nichol) review dozens of permit requests across the province daily in an administrative queue. If your title is just *"Exhibition Game"* or *"Away Game"*, approvers cannot see who you are playing without clicking through every individual permit, causing approval delays or rejected requests. Always make sure the specific opponent name is right in the title!
        *   **Tournaments:** For tournaments, follow the same clear naming standard: `Tournament - [Tournament Name]` (e.g., `Tournament - Bluenose Invitational`).
        *   **⚠️ Severe Penalty Warning:** **Failure to create an approved travel permit for an exhibition or tournament game can result in immediate Head Coach and team suspensions from Hockey Nova Scotia.**
    *   **Step 5 — Book Officials & Timekeepers (Home Games — Monday Cutoff):** Home managers are responsible for ensuring certified on-ice officials (referees/linespersons) and off-ice officials (timekeeper/scorekeeper) are assigned.
        *   **Monday Evening Deadline:** The TAMHA Referee-in-Chief (RIC) requires all game schedules for the upcoming week and weekend submitted by **Monday evening**. 
        *   **Short-Notice Games:** If booking an exhibition game on short notice after Monday, email the RIC at [ric@trurominorhockey.ca](mailto:ric@trurominorhockey.ca) *prior* to finalizing ice to confirm whether obtaining certified officials will even be possible.
    *   **Step 6 — Prepare Paper Game Sheet & Post-Game Score Entry:** Most sanctioned regular season games are scored electronically in GrayJay. However, paper game sheets are **mandatory** in four specific scenarios:
        1. Playing an out-of-league exhibition game (GrayJay does not support electronic scoring across unaffiliated leagues).
        2. Playing an opponent with no loaded GrayJay roster.
        3. Assigned timekeeper has no GrayJay credentials or mobile device experience.
        4. No cellular service or arena Wi-Fi available at the venue.
        *   **Preparation:** Pre-populate or affix printed roster stickers detailing all player names, jersey numbers, and bench staff (with full HCR numbers and coaching certification levels). Have the opposing manager affix their roster and have both Head Coaches sign before puck drop. Ensure on-ice officials sign the completed sheet at game end.
        *   **Mandatory End-of-Day Score Entry:** Using a paper game sheet does *not* excuse leaving GrayJay blank. Managers with timekeeper access must log into GrayJay and enter the final game score by **end of day** to ensure league standings and statistics remain current.
*   **Scheduling Practices & Events in GrayJay:** When entering practices or off-ice events into GrayJay, only select the venue and the "Full Ice" option if your team has exclusive use of the entire ice pad. For shared ice practices or dryland sessions, select the venue but set the ice option to **"N/A"** to prevent triggering false venue conflict warnings.
*   **Game Cancellations:** If a home game is cancelled due to weather or scheduling conflicts, immediately notify the RIC, [info@trurominorhockey.ca](mailto:info@trurominorhockey.ca), and your league scheduler so officials, timekeepers, and ice slots can be cancelled without incurring penalties.
*   **Roster Sign-offs (League Games — Mobile SOP):** Electronic game sheet sign-off is **mandatory before the timekeeper can start the game**.
    *   **Mobile Sign-Off Steps:** On your phone, log into GrayJay &rarr; Navigate to **Games** &rarr; Select your game &rarr; Click **"HOME SIGN OFF"** (or Visiting). Verify jersey numbers, mark absent players, select your starting goaltender, and check off bench staff present. Click **"SAVE"**, then **turn your phone sideways (landscape) to sign your name and save**.
    *   **Visiting Team Cooperation:** If you are the home manager, remind the visiting manager to complete their sign-off well before game time. If they experience difficulty, ask your timekeeper to assist. If a player arrives late, simply re-open the sign-off to update their attendance.
*   **🚨 Rep League vs. Tournament Conflicts & Mandatory Rescheduling Notice:**
    *   **League Schedulers Do NOT Check Tournament Dates:** Competitive league scheduling bodies (Central Minor, North Conference, NRHF) create regular season schedules independently. **They do not cross-reference or accommodate individual team tournament dates or blackout weekends.**
    *   **Immediate Cross-Referencing:** The moment your team's tournament applications are confirmed—and the moment regular season league schedules are published—the manager must cross-reference every weekend on your team calendar.
    *   **Mandatory Advance Notice to Opponents:** If a scheduled league game conflicts with an away tournament weekend, you must contact the opposing team's manager **weeks in advance** to initiate a game reschedule. Waiting until the week-of is a severe breach of minor hockey etiquette and can trigger ice cancellation charges, league rescheduling fines, or game forfeits.
*   **Tournaments & Travel:** Research sanctioned tournaments early in the season with the coaching staff:
    *   **Nova Scotia:** [Hockey NS Tournament Listings](https://hockeynovascotia.ca/admin-member/tournaments/minor-hockey-tournaments/)
    *   **New Brunswick:** [Hockey NB Sanctioned Tournaments](https://www.hnb.ca/en/)
    *   **PEI:** [Hockey PEI](https://hockeypei.com/) and individual minor hockey association websites.
*   **Travel Permits (Spordle):** Mandatory for any non-regular season game (exhibition or tournament) via [Spordle Travel Permits](https://myaccount.spordle.com/manage/permit). Must be submitted 48–72 hours in advance (1 week recommended). **Mandatory Title Format:** Always put the opponent name in the permit title (e.g., `Exhibition Game - [Opposing Team Name]`) for both Home and Away games so Hockey NS can approve promptly without queue delays.

## 4. Team Culture & "Nice to Haves"

*   **Pre-Season Parent Meeting:** Schedule the introductory parent meeting as soon as the final roster is selected. Key agenda items:
    *   **Financials & The >75% Approval Vote:** Present the drafted team operating budget and proposed tournament schedule for parental approval. Proactively explain the rep fee structure ($9,500 standard / $11,500 U13 AAA) to ease sticker shock by clarifying that it now bundles the $550 jersey fee, HNS/league fees, and two pairs of official game socks per player (no separate bills) and is split into two installments. **Mandatory Vote:** Any proposed out-of-pocket parent fee contributions or cash calls must be voted on and approved by at least a **>75% majority vote**. For C-League teams, present the two-track strategy: demonstrate how team fundraising can offset all tournament and event costs to achieve a **$0 out-of-pocket parent assessment**.
    *   **The 24-Hour Rule:** Outline the mandatory cooling-off period (parents must wait 24 hours after a game before contacting coaches/managers regarding coaching decisions or game issues). "Leave the coaching to the coaches."
    *   **Rule of Two & Dressing Room Conduct:** Strictly enforce Hockey Canada's **Rule of Two** (two screened, certified adults present in the dressing room or immediate doorway area whenever players are present). Remind families of Hockey Canada's updated policy: **players must arrive at the rink with their base layer / undergear already on**, and all cell phones/recording devices are strictly prohibited in dressing rooms.
    *   **Rink Supervision (No Unsupervised Drop-offs):** Parents **must not drop players off early or leave them unsupervised at the rink**. Arrival times should align strictly with team policy (e.g., 45 mins before games, 30 mins before practices) when screened team staff are present.
    *   **Dressing Room Music & Team Speaker:** Discuss the Head Coach's preferences for music. Designate who brings and manages the team Bluetooth speaker, and establish firm rules on **acceptable music** (clean/radio edits only, respectful volume, and muted immediately when coaches enter).
    *   **Volunteer Recruitment:** Seek volunteers for Treasurer, Safety Person, Dressing Room Monitors, and a **Designated Backup Timekeeper** (TAMHA offers comprehensive training and per-game honorarium pay for team staff or parents willing to step in as certified backup timekeepers).
*   **Team Communications & Digital Tools:** Use email and the **GrayJay Teams App** for fast updates and schedule changes:
    *   **Team Calendar "Subscribe" Button:** Instruct parents to visit their team's official page on the TAMHA website and click the **"SUBSCRIBE"** button. This automatically syncs the team's live schedule to their Apple, Google, or Outlook calendar and dynamically updates whenever practice or game times change.
    *   **GrayJay Teams App (Full Version Active):** Encourage all families to install the GrayJay Teams app for real-time team chat, schedule viewing, and **Parent Attendance RSVP** (parents can mark their child as attending or absent for upcoming practices and games).
    *   **Email Formatting:** **Always include the team's division in your email subjects** (e.g., *"U13A-Bearcats: Weekend Schedule & Rink Arrival"*). A private team Facebook group may also be created for extended family photos and livestreaming.
*   **Game Jerseys & Team Socks (Immediate Need):** Teams often schedule exhibition games within the first week or two of team selection. Email `equipment@trurominorhockey.ca` immediately to make arrangements for jersey pickup and inquire about availability of matching team socks. Assign numbers, collect deposits (unless your division receives sponsored jerseys), and sync roster numbers in GrayJay immediately so the team is game-ready.
*   **Practice Jerseys (Check with Coach):** Check in with the Head Coach early to determine practice jersey expectations. Clarify whether the team will order matching practice jerseys, whether players should supply their own, and what specific colors are required for practice drills (e.g., light vs. dark).
*   **Team Apparel & Swag:** Coordinate team apparel orders (tracksuits, warm-up hoodies, jackets) following TAMHA branding guidelines. Coordinate custom namebars if authorized.
*   **Team Events & Culture:** Book team photography (individual and team photos) early in the fall. Organize team-building activities (pizza parties, team breakfasts, attending a Truro Junior A Bearcats game). Plan an end-of-year team party and coordinate coaches’ gifts.
*   **Fundraising & Sponsorships:** Recruit a parent volunteer to lead fundraising initiatives (e.g., 50/50, bottle drives, silent auctions). Distribute the [Team Sponsorship Request Letter](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1g0c2JTK6_G58kDTBOPLDfQfnlHICo4sl/copy) to local businesses to subsidize team costs, issue official receipts using the [Sponsorship Receipt Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1c2M5fnxsjmiiY2RXzHKXxFJJzZaVOKevxBwM-IyXNPo/copy), and log all contributions directly on the Income tab of your team budget spreadsheet.
