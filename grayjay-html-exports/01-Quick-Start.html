Quick-Glance Checklist: Essential Manager Requirements

This is your bare-bones, chronological checklist for getting your team set up, compliant, and on the ice. For full details on any of these tasks, follow the links to the Detailed Guide.

Phase 1: Immediate Tasks (Days 1–3 Post-Team Selection)

HCR Staff Profile & Waiver Sign-Off Read Guide →
All bench staff must log into register.hockeycanada.ca to confirm account info and sign mandatory annual waivers (often the #1 hold-up for official team roster approvals). Any new staff without an HCR profile must register under TAMHA as a new coach/volunteer first to generate an HCR profile and begin required clinics.
GrayJay Account Setup Read Guide →
Email tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca with your Name, Position, Date of Birth, and HCR # to get your manager access set up.
Team Banking Registration Read Guide →
Submit signer details via the TAMHA Bank Signer Intake Form to request an official authorization letter from VP Finance. Do not collect parent funds until this is active. Two signers are required.
Secure Game Jerseys & Socks Read Guide →
Pick up and assign team game jerseys and secure matching team socks right away (exhibition games often start immediately). Check with Head Coach regarding practice jersey requirements (team vs. player-supplied colors).

Phase 2: Administrative Setup (Week 1)

Collect Player Medical Forms Read Guide →
Secure signed Medical Forms and keep copies in the bench binder at all times.
Identify Target Tournaments Read Guide →
Identify target tournaments, register immediately, and submit early payments to secure spots.
Jersey Deposits & Sign-outs Read Guide →
Collect $150/player (Rep) or $50/player (House). Must be refunded at year-end upon safe return.
Host Parent Meeting Read Guide →
Pitch the budget, detail the 24-Hour Rule, enforce the Rule of Two (rink/dressing room conduct; no dropping kids off early/unsupervised), align on coach music preferences (speaker & acceptable playlists), and assign volunteer roles.
Collect Forms Read Guide →
Secure signed Codes of Conduct, Social Media Policies, Bench Code of Conduct, Fair Play Policy, and Jersey Agreements.

Phase 3: Early Season Action Items (Weeks 2–4)

Verify Certifications / CRCs Read Guide →
Email info@trurominorhockey.ca to ensure all bench staff have Respect in Sport, Criminal Record Checks, and required HNS courses.
Solicit Sponsorships Read Guide →
Distribute letters to target community sponsors to help subsidize budgets.
Collect Rep Fees Read Guide →
Collect team Rep Fees ($9,500 standard / $11,500 AAA, split Nov 15 & Jan 31; includes jersey fund).

Phase 4: Ongoing / Weekly Rhythm

Roster Sign-Offs Read Guide →
Sign off on the game roster in GrayJay Leagues before puck drop.
Affiliate Tracking Read Guide →
Ensure both Head Coaches confirm when an affiliate is called up, email tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca with player details, track their total game count (max 10).
Travel Permits (Spordle) Read Guide →
Submit permits for ALL non-regular season games (Exhibition and Tournaments) well in advance.
Injury Reports Read Guide →
If a player requires medical attention, complete and submit a Hockey Nova Scotia Injury Report.
Weekly Updates Read Guide →
Send a weekly email/GrayJay Team chat with the upcoming schedule, arrival times, and action items.
