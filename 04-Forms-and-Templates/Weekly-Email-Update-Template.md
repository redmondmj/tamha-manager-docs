# TAMHA Weekly Team Update Email Template & Setup Guide

This guide provides the official weekly communication template and workflow for team managers. 

By utilizing the official association Google Workspace account (`u13amgr@trurominorhockey.ca`) instead of the GrayJay bulk emailer, you ensure:
1. **High Deliverability & Trust:** Senders from `@trurominorhockey.ca` bypass spam/promotions folders and establish authority.
2. **Two-Way Threading:** Direct parent replies land in your manager inbox where they can be searched, labeled, and tracked.
3. **Mobile-Responsive Layouts:** Clean formatting without GrayJay's WYSIWYG glitches or microscopic fixed-width tables on smartphones.
4. **Privacy Protection:** Sending via BCC prevents accidental "Reply-All" storms and safeguards parent contact lists.

---

## 🛠️ One-Time Setup in Gmail

### 1. Enable Gmail Templates
1. Log into your official manager account at [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com).
2. Click the **Settings gear icon (⚙️)** in the top right > **See all settings**.
3. Navigate to the **Advanced** tab.
4. Under **Templates**, select **Enable**.
5. Scroll to the bottom and click **Save Changes**.

### 2. Create the Parent Distribution List (Contact Group)
1. Go to [contacts.google.com](https://contacts.google.com) while signed into your manager account.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Create label** and name it: `U13A Parents 2026-2027` (or your division).
3. Add all player parent/guardian email addresses to this label.
4. When composing your weekly email:
   - Put your own address (`u13amgr@trurominorhockey.ca`) in the **To:** field.
   - Put the label `U13A Parents 2026-2027` in the **Bcc:** field.
   - Put the Head Coach / Bench Staff in the **Cc:** or **Bcc:** field as appropriate.

### 3. Save the Reusable Template
1. Click **Compose** in Gmail.
2. Paste the template content below into the message body.
3. Set the subject line to: `[U13A Bearcats] Weekly Update — [Week of Month Day]`
4. Click the **three dots (More options)** icon at the bottom right of the compose window.
5. Hover over **Templates** > **Save draft as template** > **Save as new template**.
6. Name it: `Weekly Update Master Template`.

## ⏱️ Recommended Weekly Routine

| Day | Task |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sunday** | Check GrayJay and TAMHA Master Ice Schedule for the upcoming week. Cross-reference venue and times. |
| **Monday** | Quick touchpoint with Head Coach for any specific practice focuses, arrival times, or dressing room notes. |
| **Tuesday (Morning)** | Open Gmail > Compose > Insert `Weekly Update Master Template` > Update Schedule & Action Items > Send to parent BCC list. |
| **Thurs/Fri** | Collect any outstanding payments/forms at practice; ensure GrayJay roster sign-off is ready for weekend games. |
| **Sat/Sun** | Game day execution. Use GrayJay Teams chat only for urgent day-of announcements (e.g., room change, weather delay). |
