Team Managers Fill Out the 60-Second Form

Eliminates scattered email threads, missing phone numbers, and nickname confusion.

👉 TAMHA Team Bank Account Signer Intake Form (Live Link)
  1. Select Team: Pick the team from the official directory (covers all 28 official TAMHA teams U7–U18, plus "Other" for newly formed teams).
  2. Enter Signers: Provide full legal names, positions, cell phone numbers, and email addresses for:
    • Primary Signer: Mandatory signing officer (Manager, Treasurer, or Coach).
    • Secondary Signer: Mandatory dual signer (required by TAMHA policy & Mosaik).
    • Third Signer: Optional backup signer.
  3. Instant Synchronization: As soon as the manager taps Submit, the data immediately streams into the Form Responses 1 tab of the Master Finance Sheet.

1-Click Document Generation in Google Sheets

No manual Word document editing or copy-pasting while traveling.

👉 TAMHA Team Banking & Finance Master Dashboard (Live Link)
  1. Open the master spreadsheet. In the top Google Sheets menu bar, click:
    🏒 TAMHA Banking → ⚡ Generate Bank Letters for Pending Teams
Figure 2: In-browser 1-click letter generator in the Google Sheets toolbar.
  1. The automated engine runs in under 3 seconds:
    • Copies the official TAMHA letterhead template with the high-resolution Bearcats crest.
    • Merges team name, account number, legal signer names, roles, emails, and cell numbers.
    • Generates the Google Doc in Drive and automatically writes the direct link back into Column X (Authorization Letter).
Figure 3: Generated authorization letter URL automatically saved in the master dashboard.

Open the Letter & Share Directly with Blake Giroux

Seamless handoff to Mosaik Credit Union for DocuSign signing officer execution.

Option A: Direct Drive Sharing (Recommended — Fastest, Zero Attachments)

  1. Click the generated document link in the sheet to open the letter in Google Docs.
  2. In the top right corner, click the blue Share button.
  3. In the text box, type Blake Giroux (BGiroux@mosaikcu.ca).
  4. Set access to Viewer, and click Send.
Figure 4: Securely granting Viewer access to Blake Giroux directly within Google Docs.

✔ Done! Blake instantly accesses the official letter and initiates remote DocuSign signing for team staff.

Option B: Download & Email as PDF (If Mosaik Requires an Attachment)

  1. In the open Google Doc, click File → Download → PDF Document (.pdf).
  2. Attach the downloaded PDF to an email addressed to BGiroux@mosaikcu.ca.
