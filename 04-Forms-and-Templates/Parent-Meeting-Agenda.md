## 3. Communication & Protocols
*   **The 24-Hour Rule:** If you have an issue regarding a game or coaching decision, you must wait 24 hours before contacting the coaching staff or manager. This allows emotions to cool and leads to productive conversations.
*   **Dressing Room Conduct & Supervision (Rule of Two):** Reiteration of the **Rule of Two / Two-Deep Method** (there must always be two screened adults present in the dressing room when players are present).
    *   **No Unsupervised Drop-offs:** Parents must **not** drop players off early or leave them unsupervised at the rink. Arrival times must strictly follow team rules (e.g., 30–45 mins before games, 20–30 mins before practices) when screened team staff are on site. Players are not permitted in dressing rooms without staff supervision.
    *   **Cell Phone Ban:** Reiterate strict ban on cell phones and recording devices in dressing rooms at all times.
*   **Dressing Room Music & Team Speaker:** Outline the Head Coach's music policy. Designate who is responsible for bringing and managing the team Bluetooth speaker. Establish clear rules on **acceptable music** (clean/radio edits only, appropriate volume, and music turned off when coaches enter to speak).
*   **Jerseys & Socks:** Outline distribution and deposit requirements for game jerseys ($150 Rep / $50 House; not required if your division receives sponsored jerseys) and matching team socks (needed immediately for upcoming exhibition games). *   **Weekly Updates:** The manager will send an email every [Tuesday] with the upcoming weekend schedule.

## 4. Player Safety & Equipment Standards (Hockey Canada Guidelines)
*   **CSA-Certified Helmets & Expiration Dates:** Referees and safety reps strictly enforce helmet compliance. Every player and goalie helmet must display a valid, legible **CSA certification sticker** that has not expired.
    *   **Hardware Inspection:** Check that ear loops and chin straps are intact, properly fastened, and unaltered.
    *   **Cracks & Modifications:** Helmets with cracked shells, missing screws/hardware, non-approved paint, or unauthorized stickers are strictly prohibited and cannot be worn on the ice. Any damaged or expired helmet must be replaced immediately.
*   **BNQ-Certified Neck Laceration Protectors (Throat Protectors):** Mandatory for all players and goaltenders at all times during practices, warm-ups, and games. Must fit snugly around the neck and cannot be cut, trimmed, or altered in any manner.
*   **Mouthguards:** Properly fitted mouthguards are required/recommended per HNS regulations.
*   **Official Equipment Resource:** Parents are encouraged to review [Hockey Canada Equipment Essentials](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/safety/essentials/equipment) for visual fit guides, certification marks, and maintenance standards.

## 5. Team Finances & Budget

*   **Cash Handling & Tracking (Laurie's "Witness Rule"):** If submitting cash payments for team fees, apparel, or socks:
    *   **Always Have a Witness:** Cash is always counted and accepted in the presence of a second team official (e.g., Manager + Treasurer or Coach).
    *   **Immediate Tracking & Receipt:** The payment will be logged immediately in the team tracker, and a written receipt or confirmation email will be provided on the spot.
    *   **Prompt Deposit:** All collected funds are placed into a labeled envelope and deposited promptly into the team bank account—never kept as loose cash.
*   **Fundraising/Sponsorship:** Discuss plans to offset costs (e.g., 50/50, bottle drives, corporate sponsorships).
