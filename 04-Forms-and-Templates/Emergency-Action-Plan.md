## The Three EAP Roles
Prior to the start of every game or practice, the Safety Person must assign the following three roles. In the event of a significant injury, these roles take immediate effect.

### 1. Charge Person (Usually Safety Person or Coach)
*   **Role:** The most qualified person to handle injury assessments.
*   **Tasks:**
    *   Takes control of an emergency situation until medical personnel arrive.
    *   Assesses the severity of the injury.
    *   Signals for the Call Person to contact 911 if required.
    *   Does NOT move the injured player unless the environment poses an immediate threat to life.

### 2. Call Person
*   **Role:** The person responsible for contacting emergency services.
*   **Tasks:**
    *   Knows the exact location and address of the arena (including the specific rink pad if in a multi-pad complex).
    *   Possesses an active, fully charged cell phone.
    *   Calls 911 upon the signal from the Charge Person.
    *   Provides dispatcher with location, nature of injury, and quickest access point to the ice.
    *   Reports back to the Charge Person that an ambulance is en route.

### 3. Control Person
*   **Role:** The person responsible for crowd and player control.
*   **Tasks:**
    *   Ensures players go to their benches and DO NOT crowd the injured player.
    *   Keeps concerned parents off the ice unless explicitly requested by the Charge Person.
    *   Secures the quickest route to the ice for paramedics (opening ice doors, clearing pathways, removing debris).
    *   Waits at the arena entrance to guide the ambulance attendants directly to the injured player.

## Important Information (Bench Binder)
*   Ensure **Player Medical Forms** (Hockey Canada forms) are present in the bench binder at all times. Give the injured player's form directly to paramedics upon their arrival.
*   **Injury Report:** Following any medical incident, the Safety Person or Manager must complete a Hockey Nova Scotia Injury Report form immediately.
