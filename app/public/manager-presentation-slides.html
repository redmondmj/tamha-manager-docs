Financial Compliance

Team Banking: Mosaik Credit Union

100% digital, paperless account authorization — no in-person branch visits required.

  • 1
    Step 1: Submit Signer Intake Form Manager or Treasurer enters legal names, roles, cell phones, and emails for two signing officers.
  • 2
    Step 2: Corporate Authorization Letter VP Finance (Joe Zappia) generates and delivers the official letter directly to Mosaik commercial services. (Managers do not handle letters!)
  • 3
    Step 3: DocuSign Digital Signature Cards Mosaik emails the signature package via DocuSign directly to both signers to review and e-sign from their phones.
  • 4
    Step 4: PAC Codes & Dual-Authorization Mosaik emails account details & Personal Access Codes. Dual-authorization is strictly enforced on all e-transfers.
✅ Do Not Collect Parent Funds Yet:

Wait until your Mosaik account credentials are live before collecting parent fee e-transfers. Never co-mingle team funds into personal bank accounts.

Google Drive Integration

Team Operating Budgets (Pre-Populated)

Your team budget is already generated and waiting for you in Google Drive — zero blank spreadsheet setup.

  • 📁
    Where to Find Your Budget: Log into your Google account and open Google Drive > "Shared with me" — your team's official spreadsheet is already active with Editor access for your Manager and Treasurer!
  • ⚙
    Pre-Populated & Formulas Linked: Pre-configured with division details, roster counter, and Rep fee schedule ($9,500 Rep / $11,500 AAA / $0 House).
  • 💡
    Explaining Rep Fees (No Sticker Shock!): Proactively explain to parents that the Rep fee bundles the $550 jersey fund, HNS league fees, and 2 pairs of game socks per player! Split 50/50: Nov 30 & Jan 31.
✨ Already started an Excel sheet? Don't panic!

Please don't re-type it! Simply email what you have to TAMHA Finance (vpfinance@trurominorhockey.ca) or Matt Redmond and we will merge your numbers into the official format for you in 5 minutes.
