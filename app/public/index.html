Quick-Glance Checklist: Essential Manager Requirements

This is your bare-bones, chronological checklist for getting your team set up, compliant, and on the ice. For full details on any of these tasks, follow the links to the Detailed Guide.

Phase 1: Immediate Tasks (Days 1-3 Post-Team Selection)

Item Name Description Details
Team Staff Onboarding & GrayJay Access The Head Coach is assigned an official TAMHA email account first and asked to confirm their designated Team Manager. Once confirmed, provide your details (Full Name, Date of Birth, and HCR #) to receive your official manager email account and GrayJay manager access. (Roster Guardrails: Rosters sync from registrations; never manually add/release players or add extra admins. Tell parents to add contacts via GrayJay Pay). Read Guide →
Submit Full Bench Staff & APs to TAMHA Email your complete coaching and bench staff list (Assistant Coaches, Safety Reps, Trainers) with full legal names, assigned roles, D.O.B., and HCR #s directly to Comms & Tech at tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca ASAP. Managers cannot add staff directly in GrayJay—TAMHA must link them to your roster. (Rep teams: Also submit your list of AP players to be linked). Read Guide →
HCR Staff Profile & Waiver Sign-Off All bench staff must log into register.hockeycanada.ca to confirm account info and sign mandatory annual waivers (often the #1 hold-up for official team roster approvals). Any new staff without an HCR profile must register under TAMHA as a new coach/volunteer first to generate an HCR profile and begin required clinics. Read Guide →
Team Banking Registration Submit signer details via the TAMHA Bank Signer Intake Form. VP Finance generates and submits the official authorization letter directly to Mosaik Credit Union on your behalf (managers do not send letters or visit the branch). Signature cards are completed digitally via DocuSign. Important: All signers must complete DocuSign before online banking credentials can be issued (one missing signature holds up the entire account). Do not collect parent funds until this is active. Two signers are required. (Cash Handling Rule: Always count and track cash with a second team official as a witness, issue immediate receipts, and deposit promptly). Read Guide →
Secure Game Jerseys & Socks Email equipment@trurominorhockey.ca immediately to make arrangements for jersey pickup and inquire about availability of matching game socks (exhibition games often start immediately). Assign jerseys, collect deposits, and check with Head Coach regarding practice jersey requirements. Read Guide →

Phase 2: Administrative Setup (Week 1)

Item Name Description Details
Collect Player Medical Forms Secure signed Medical Forms and keep copies in the bench binder at all times. Read Guide →
Identify Target Tournaments Identify target tournaments, register immediately, and submit early payments to secure spots. Read Guide →
Jersey Deposits & Sign-outs Collect $150/player (Rep) or $50/player (House). Must be refunded at year-end upon safe return.
(Note: Some divisions receive sponsored jerseys and do not collect deposits. If unsure, confirm with your division rep or email info@trurominorhockey.ca.)		 Read Guide →
Host Parent Meeting & Budget Approval Vote Pitch the budget (any out-of-pocket parent fees/cash calls require a >75% majority approval vote; present the $0 out-of-pocket fundraising plan for C-League), detail the 24-Hour Rule, enforce Dressing Room Policy (base layers on arrival & Rule of Two), share the calendar "SUBSCRIBE" link and GrayJay Teams App (chat & RSVP), review equipment safety (CSA stickers, BNQ neck guards), and recruit volunteers (including a Designated Backup Timekeeper with TAMHA pay). Read Guide →
Player Equipment & Safety Verification Review player gear against Hockey Canada standards: inspect helmets for valid unexpired CSA stickers, intact ear loops, and secure chin straps, and verify every player has a BNQ-certified throat protector. Read Guide →
Collect Forms Secure signed Codes of Conduct, Social Media Policies, Bench Code of Conduct, Fair Play Policy, and Jersey Agreements. Read Guide →
Player Transfers to TAMHA Follow up with parents of players who recently moved to the Truro area and played for another minor hockey association last season. Parents must submit the Parent Declaration Form to info@trurominorhockey.ca to transfer their HCR profile to TAMHA (unfinalized transfers hold up official roster approvals). Read Guide →

Phase 3: Early Season Action Items (Weeks 2-4)

Item Name Description Details
Verify Certifications/CRCs (Strict Deadline: Dec 1st) Email info@trurominorhockey.ca to ensure all bench staff have Respect in Sport, Criminal Record Checks / VSCs (done at local police station with TAMHA letter and 2 IDs), and required HNS courses before December 1st. Read Guide →
Audit League Schedule for Tournament Conflicts (Rep Teams) League schedulers (Central Minor, North Conference, NRHF) do not check tournament dates. Cross-reference your league schedule against tournament weekends immediately and notify opposing managers weeks in advance to reschedule conflicts. Read Guide →
Solicit Sponsorships Distribute letters to target community sponsors to help subsidize budgets. Read Guide →
Collect Rep Fees Collect team Rep Fees ($9,500 standard / $11,500 U13 AAA, split Nov 30 & Jan 31; includes mandatory jersey fund). Read Guide →
Exhibition Game Setup (Out-of-League) For exhibition games outside your league, follow the 6-step SOP: book opponent via GrayJay Central, secure ice, enter as "Out of League" / "To Be Announced" (or Scheduled Event for away games), submit mandatory Spordle Travel Permit 1 week prior (must title as: Exhibition Game – [Opposing Team Name] per Hockey NS; unapproved permits risk suspensions), request refs/timekeepers via RIC before Monday evening cutoff, and pre-fill physical paper game sheets (GrayJay doesn't support live stats for out-of-league play; enter score in GrayJay by end of day). Phase 4: Ongoing / Weekly Rhythm

Item Name Description Details
Roster Sign-Offs (Paperless SOP) Sign off on the game roster in GrayJay mobile before puck drop (select game → Home/Visiting Sign Off → choose goalie/bench staff → turn phone sideways to sign). Mark late players absent or update as needed. Read Guide →
Affiliate Tracking & Roster Rules Ensure both Head Coaches confirm when an affiliate is called up, email tamhaweb@trurominorhockey.ca with player details, change AP from "NOT DRESSED" to "DRESSED" with jersey number, verify APs do not exceed regular roster size cap, and track total game count (max 10). Read Guide →
Travel Permits (Spordle) Submit permits for ALL non-regular season games (Exhibition and Tournaments) 48–72h in advance. Mandatory Title Format: Always put the opponent name in the permit title (e.g., Exhibition Game – [Opposing Team Name] or Tournament – [Name]) for both Home and Away games so Hockey NS can approve without queue delays. Read Guide →
Injury Reports If a player requires medical attention, complete and submit a Hockey Nova Scotia Injury Report. Read Guide →
Weekly Updates Send a weekly email/GrayJay Team chat with the upcoming schedule, arrival times, and action items. Remind parents to RSVP attendance on the GrayJay Teams app. Read Guide →
